The NerdSpace help desk

Let’s get you back to your people.

Account trouble, a safety concern, or a question about the app? Here is where to start.

Contact support

Write to mtheophilus565@gmail.com. Include your @handle, device type, app version and a short description. Remove private information from screenshots. Never send a password, verification code or private recording.

Email NerdSpace

Safety and moderation

Use the menu on a post or profile to report content or block an account. Give a clear reason so moderators can investigate. Appeals are available in the app. If you cannot sign in, email support with the affected @handle and what happened.

For concerns involving child exploitation, threats, or copyright, identify the relevant content or account in your message. Do not send illegal images or videos as attachments.

Quick answers

I haven’t received my verification email.

Check spam and promotions folders, confirm the address you typed, and use the resend option in the app. Contact support from the affected address if it remains unresolved.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings in NerdSpace and choose Delete account. If you cannot use the app, follow the web account-deletion instructions.

How long do statuses last?

Statuses disappear from view 24 hours after posting. Expired media is processed by the cleanup service; removal from storage can take longer than visibility expiry.

What should I do about an incorrect spoiler label?

Report the post and describe the incorrect label. Spoiler ratings are chosen by authors; they are not a guarantee that every surprise will be filtered.

Can I buy NerdSpace Plus?

Public store subscriptions are not open yet. Administrator-granted Plus access can be used during testing. This website does not collect payments. Contact support about an access issue.

Privacy questions

Read our privacy policy for information about data, service providers, visibility and deletion. You can send access, correction, or deletion requests to the support address.
