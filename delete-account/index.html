Account and data help

Your account. Your choice.

You can request deletion of your NerdSpace account and its associated data without installing the app again.

In the app

  1. Sign in to NerdSpace and open Settings.
  2. Choose Delete account and read the confirmation.
  3. Complete the requested reauthentication and keep the app open while the staged cleanup runs. Contact support if it stops before completion.

From the web

Email mtheophilus565@gmail.com with the subject NerdSpace account deletion request. Send it from the email registered to your account, include your @handle, and state that you want your account and associated data deleted.

We verify account ownership before acting. If you cannot access the registered address, describe that issue so support can explain the available verification steps. Never include your password or verification codes.

Request deletion by email

What deletion covers

Deletion processes your account, profile, authored content, owned uploads, private messages you authored, notification registrations and account-linked activity through staged cleanup. Shared conversation references can display “Deleted account”; other people’s messages and content are not necessarily deleted.

Security, moderation and audit records may be retained where needed for abuse investigations or legal obligations. Backups and pending cleanup can take longer. Support can explain the status of your request; we do not advertise an unverified completion deadline.

For a request to delete particular data while keeping your account, describe the data in an email. See the privacy policy for further information.
