Make room for each other

Big feelings. Real people.

NerdSpace is an independent community for anime and movie fans aged 18 or older. It is not affiliated with the publishers of the titles discussed.

Keep people safe

No harassment, threats, hate, doxxing, impersonation, spam, child sexual abuse or exploitation, or illegal content. Do not share malware, pirated episodes or links that distribute unlawful material. Debate ideas and stories without targeting the people behind them.

Share content responsibly

Only upload media you own or have permission to share. You remain responsible for your account and content. Use spoiler labels honestly. Room moderators can set room rules within these platform rules; platform administrators can investigate reports, remove content and restrict accounts.

Names of anime and films identify the subjects of independent fan discussions. Original NerdSpace illustrations are decorative and do not claim publisher sponsorship. YouTube trailers load through the provider’s player when requested.

Your account and access

Use an email address you control, verify it before posting or messaging, and protect your password. Free and Plus accounts have different posting allowances and room-management privileges. Administrator grants are separate from paid subscriptions. Store purchases become available when the relevant annual product is available in your region.

Annual Plus subscription

Plus has one annual plan, automatically renewing each year until canceled. The US base price is $12 per year ($1 per month equivalent); there is no monthly purchase option. Apple and Google show the actual localized annual price and applicable taxes before you confirm payment. Annual web checkout through Paystack is GHS 139.80 per year and renews at that annual price until canceled.

Cancel before your next renewal through your original Apple/Google subscription settings, or use Manage renewal on the web membership page for Paystack. Canceling keeps access through the paid period. Deleting a NerdSpace account does not cancel recurring billing. Use Restore purchases in the app to recover a supported purchase on your NerdSpace account. Keep access to that account; sharing store purchases across unrelated NerdSpace accounts is not supported.

Refund requests for native purchases are handled under the relevant store’s rules; for web billing contact support with the payment reference, never card details. Applicable consumer rights are unaffected. If access is restricted for abuse, contact support about both access and any ongoing renewal. Higher limits remain subject to fair use and platform safety rules.

Moderation and appeals

Report harmful posts or accounts through the app’s menu, and use blocking to control contact. Administrative actions provide notifications where supported; use the appeal feature or contact support if you believe a restriction is mistaken. Do not send illegal content to support as an attachment.

Contact and copyright

For support, copyright concerns or safety reports, email mtheophilus565@gmail.com. Identify the affected post/account and explain the concern, with enough information for a response. See Privacy and Account deletion for data choices.
