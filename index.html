Get NerdSpace
on Android.

The public Google Play release is still pending. The closed beta is available to testers whose Google Play country is set to Ghana.

Use the same Google account

Sign in with the same account in Google Groups, on the beta invite page, and in the Play Store on your phone.

  1. 1

    Join the Google Group

    Open NerdSpace Android Testers and select Join group. If you’re asked to request membership, wait for approval before continuing.

    Open Google Group (opens in a new tab)
  2. 2

    Accept the beta invite

    Once you’re a group member, open the invite and select Become a tester.

    Open beta invite (opens in a new tab)
  3. 3

    Install from Google Play

    After opting in, open the tester listing on your Android phone and tap Install.

    Open in Google Play
What if Google Play says the app is unavailable?

Check that your Google Group membership is active, the invite says you’re a tester, and your Play Store account is set to Ghana. If you just opted in, give Google Play a few minutes and try again. Contact NerdSpace support if you’re still stuck.
